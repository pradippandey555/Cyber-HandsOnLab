# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free learning environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume positive intent.
- Focus feedback on ideas and outcomes.
- Support beginner learners and diverse backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Doxxing or disclosure of private information
- Sharing malicious or illegal instructions for unauthorized access

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments/contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
