# Safety and Legal Guidelines

## Mandatory Rules
- Perform testing only on systems you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.
- Follow all applicable laws, policies, and terms of service.
- Never use course content for unauthorized access or disruption.

## Responsible Conduct
- Keep evidence handling professional and accurate.
- Protect credentials and sensitive data.
- Use least privilege in all environments.

## Incident Handling in Labs
- If you accidentally expose resources, isolate and remediate immediately.
- Document mistakes and corrective actions as learning outcomes.
