# Assessment and Grading

## Weighting
- Module quizzes: 30%
- Lab task completion: 30%
- Artifact quality: 20%
- Reporting quality: 20%

## Minimum Standards
- 70% overall score
- All mandatory modules complete
- Capstone pass required

## Review Criteria
- Technical correctness
- Evidence quality
- Clarity of analysis
- Practical remediation recommendations
