# Capstone Guide

## Scenario
A simulated multi-stage enterprise incident requiring detection, triage, containment, eradication, and recovery.

## Required Outputs
- Incident timeline
- IOC and TTP mapping
- Root cause analysis
- Containment and eradication plan
- Executive summary
- Lessons learned and improvement plan

## Evaluation Focus
- Speed and quality of triage
- Evidence-backed decisions
- Communication quality for technical and business audiences
